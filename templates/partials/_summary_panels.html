Details

Raw Sets are planned sets credited to each muscle. Total credits the primary muscle at 100%, secondary at 50% and tertiary at 25%. Credits add when one muscle appears in several roles. Direct Only counts primary sets at 100%.

Effective Sets = credited raw sets × effort factor × rep range factor. This table classifies volume using effective sets.

Effort factor uses Reps In Reserve (RIR), or 10 minus RPE when only RPE is available. RIR of 0–1 earns 100%, 2–3 earns 85%, 4–5 earns 70% and 6 or more earns 55%. Missing effort defaults to 100%.

Rep range factor uses average reps. Below 6 earns 85%, 6 through 20 earns 100%, over 20 through 30 earns 85% and over 30 earns 70%. A missing or nonpositive average defaults to 100%.

Training Volume Formula for each exercise is Effective Sets × Avg Reps × Weight. The table sums those volumes per muscle.
